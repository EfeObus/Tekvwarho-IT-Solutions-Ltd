TEKVWA Money

An automated personal finance app built for Canada and Nigeria. Instead of manual tracking, TEKVWA Money connects directly to your bank account, analyzes where your money is going, detects spending patterns, and alerts you to hidden financial leakages.

Download TEKVWA-Money-v1.0.apk

Android only, version 1.0

Canada

Live direct bank sync via Plaid is fully functional. Connect your Canadian bank account right away.

Nigeria

Direct bank sync (via Mono) is currently in internal test mode and will be enabled soon. For now, upload bank statements or track transactions manually.

How to Install (Android APK)

A few extra steps since this isn't on the Play Store yet

  1. Download & Save Download the TEKVWA-Money-v1.0.apk file above to your phone.
  2. Open the File Open it from your downloads notification, or your phone's "Downloads" / "My Files" folder.
  3. Allow Unknown Sources Android will show a prompt stating the file is from an unknown source. Tap "Settings" on the prompt and toggle ON "Allow from this source."
    Samsung users: if install is blocked, go to Settings > Security and privacy > Auto Blocker and turn it OFF temporarily.
  4. Install Tap "Back" and then "Install." If Google Play Protect shows a popup, tap "Install anyway."
  5. Register Open the app, tap Register, and create your account using the email verification code sent to you.
  6. Choose Your Country Select Canada or Nigeria. This sets your default currency (CAD or NGN) and primary features, and cannot be changed later — pick the country matching your bank account.
